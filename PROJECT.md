# PROJECT.md — EduConsultPro Project Specifications

## 1. Project Goal
Develop an end-to-end institutional CRM on Salesforce to manage the entire student lifecycle — from prospective lead inquiry, counselor evaluation, enrollment approval, and batch assignment, to real-time analytics.

## 2. Key Domains
- **Admissions Pipeline**: Custom Lead conversion logic mapping prospective students directly to `Contact` and `Student_Application__c` records.
- **Academic Batching**: Conflict-prevention validation triggers for instructor schedules and classroom physical capacity.
- **LWC Interfaces**: Custom responsive interfaces for students and counselors.
- **Analytics Dashboards**: Lead Conversion Rate, Counselor Resolution Latency, and Monthly Enrollment Metrics.
